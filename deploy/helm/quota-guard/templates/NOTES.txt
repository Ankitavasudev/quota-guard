quota-guard has been deployed.

1. Verify the operator is running:
   kubectl get pods -n {{ .Values.namespace }}

2. Create a ResourceBudget:
   kubectl apply -f https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Ankitavasudev/quota-guard/main/config/samples/resourcebudget.yaml

3. Watch status:
   kubectl get resourcebudgets -A

4. Metrics are exposed on port 8080:
   kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Values.namespace }} svc/{{ include "quota-guard.fullname" . }}-metrics 8080:8080
